# Native notes surface

Mode: Operate / Read. Visual authority: Omarchy's installed Color and Style
tokens, native QML controls, and the user's frost/scale/fade direction.

The source window's rectangle becomes a calm writing surface. Concrete scope
tabs sit above the note, a compact toolbar offers preview, collection
and settings, and a quiet footer reports persistence. Writing receives the
space; no dashboard cards, promotional copy, or decorative indicators.

Use 180–220 ms eased frost/fade with approximately 1% scale, reduce spatial
motion when requested, and keep the editor readable against an opaque fallback.
Visibility is independent of focus. The surface never intercepts neighboring
applications' clicks or keeps an exclusive keyboard grab.
